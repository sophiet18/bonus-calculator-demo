Transparent fictional rules

  • Quality below 90: no bonus.
  • Quality at least 90 and revenue below HK$100,000.00: no bonus.
  • Quality at least 90 and revenue HK$100,000.00 to below HK$500,000.00: 8%.
  • Quality at least 90 and revenue HK$500,000.00 or more: 12%.

The rate applies to all eligible revenue. Each bonus is rounded to the nearest cent, half-cent ties up; the total sums those rounded bonuses.
